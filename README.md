# Fixkar

Fixkar is a technology-enabled service marketplace that connects customers with verified professionals for home repair, maintenance, renovation, and related services.

## Project Overview

Fixkar is being developed as a MERN-stack platform where customers can discover and hire professionals for services such as:

- Electrician
- Plumber
- Carpenter
- Painter
- Mason
- Labour
- Civil & Renovation Services
- AC and Ro Services

The platform is designed to manage the complete service journey, including professional onboarding, customer bookings, service management, payments, notifications, ratings, and reviews.
