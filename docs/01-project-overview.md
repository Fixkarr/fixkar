# Fixkar — Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

Fixkar is a technology-enabled service marketplace developed by Fixkar Smart Solutions Private Limited.

The platform connects customers with professionals who provide home repair, maintenance, renovation, and related services.

## 2. Problem Statement

Customers often face difficulty finding reliable professionals for home services.

Professionals also face challenges such as:

- Finding new customers
- Managing service requests
- Building trust and reputation
- Managing bookings
- Receiving payments
- Maintaining customer relationships

Fixkar aims to provide a structured platform for both customers and professionals.

## 3. Solution

Fixkar provides a platform where:

Customer
→ Selects a service
→ Finds professionals
→ Creates a booking
→ Pays applicable charges
→ Receives the service
→ Rates the professional

Professional
→ Registers
→ Gets verified
→ Creates a professional profile
→ Receives booking requests
→ Accepts the booking
→ Provides the service
→ Receives earnings

## 4. Main User Roles

### Customer

Customers can:

- Register and log in
- Search for services and professionals
- View professional profiles
- Create bookings
- Track bookings
- Make payments
- Rate and review professionals

### Professional

Professionals can:

- Register on the platform
- Submit verification information
- Manage their profile
- Manage availability
- Receive booking requests
- Accept or decline requests
- Complete services
- Track earnings
- Receive ratings and reviews

### Admin

Administrators can:

- Manage customers
- Manage professionals
- Verify professionals
- Manage services
- Manage bookings
- Monitor platform activity
- Manage payments and platform fees
- Handle complaints and disputes

## 5. Core Services

Fixkar is designed to support services such as:

- Electrician
- Plumber
- Carpenter
- Painter
- Mason
- Labour
- Civil and renovation services

The service system is designed so that additional service categories can be added in the future.
